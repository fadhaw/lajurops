**Duplicate tasks, Rescheduled and Canceled statuses, and a default workspace per user.** Hourly Workload
and Weekly Commitment also stop showing people from workspaces you can't see. Two migrations (019, 020),
applied automatically at startup; no configuration changes. LajurOps is now under the **Apache License 2.0**.

## Duplicate a task

A new **copy** button in the task dialog, next to delete, copies a task together with everything inside it:
a project with its daily and hourly tasks, or a daily task with its hourly tasks. The copy sits next to the
original (same parent) and opens straight away, with a **Back** bar to the original.

- **Kept:** title (the top copy ends in "(copy)"), description, priority, owners, dates, estimates, category
  and environment.
- **Fresh:** status To Do, no progress, actual hours or comments; runbook steps are copied unticked.
- **Projects** get their own copy of their environments, and the tasks inside point at the copies.
- **Dependencies** between copied tasks are copied; links to tasks outside the copy are not.
- Only the top copy sends an "assigned" notification, so copying a big project doesn't flood anyone.

## Rescheduled and Canceled

Daily and hourly tasks have two more statuses, with their own board columns (the board now scrolls
sideways):

- **Rescheduled** (orange) is still open work, like To Do: it counts toward progress and gets reminders.
- **Canceled** (red) closes a task without finishing it:
  - it stops blocking the tasks that wait for it, and their owners get the usual "unblocked" notice;
  - it's left out of project progress (bars, "1/3 done", the project's percentage), Hourly Workload,
    Weekly Commitment and reminders;
  - it's struck through and folded away with the done tasks.

Projects take neither status: their status still comes from their tasks.

## Default workspace per user

Admins can pick a **Default workspace** in a user's **Edit** dialog (any workspace the user can see, or All
workspaces). **Hourly Workload** and the **Team** view of **Weekly Commitment** open on it for that user,
who can still switch. If the user later loses access to it, it's ignored.

## Fixed: names from other workspaces

Hourly Workload and Weekly Commitment listed every active user, so someone with access to one workspace
saw the names of people who only work in another. With a workspace selected, or for a user who can see
only some workspaces, they now list the people who can see those workspaces, plus anyone with tasks in
them. Admins with **All workspaces** still see everyone.

Admins count as members of a workspace like anyone else: through their workspace role, or
`DEFAULT_WORKSPACE_ROLE` when none is set. To keep an admin off a workspace's lists, give them **No access**
there.

## Notes

- **Migrations:** `019_default_workspace` adds `users.default_workspace_id`; `020_rescheduled_canceled`
  allows the new statuses and stops projects from taking them. Both are additive.
- **API:** `POST /api/tasks/{id}/duplicate` (editors) returns the top copy. `status` accepts `rescheduled` and
  `canceled` for daily and hourly tasks (`400` for projects). `GET /api/me` has `default_workspace_id`;
  admins set it with `PUT /api/admin/users/{id}/default-workspace { workspace_id }` (`""` for all), and
  `GET …/access` returns it.
- **License:** Apache 2.0 instead of MIT. `NOTICE` carries the copyright line and ships in the release
  archives next to `LICENSE`.
